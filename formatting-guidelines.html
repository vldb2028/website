VLDB 2027: Formatting Guidelines

General Information

All papers submitted to PVLDB and the VLDB Conference for review, irrespective of track, must adhere strictly to the PVLDB template detailed here. Any deviation (incl. omissions of mandatory blocks, such as copyright, reference format, artifact, etc.) will be liable to desk rejection of the paper. The PVLDB style template is based on the ACM style template and is updated regularly. Here is the current PVLDB template (for Latex and Word), including the style file and a sample file, as a zip file and as an overleaf project (for Latex):
